Privacy Policy

How Lynctel collects, manages, uses, and safeguards your information across our platform.

1. Information We Collect

To offer a streamlined marketplace experience, Lynctel processes details when you sign up, build a storefront, or complete a transaction:

Identity: Legal name, brand/merchant tags, and business identifiers.

Contact: Email addresses, phone numbers, and delivery/billing addresses.

Technical: IP addresses, browser type, device identifiers, and session cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used for targeted operational functions:

Fulfillment & Operations

Processing orders, enabling vendor dashboards, and maintaining storefront layouts.

Communication

Order alerts, security verifications, system updates, and support assistance.

3. Security Infrastructure

Data storage runs inside restricted, authenticated environments protected by SSL/TLS encryption across all transmissions. Local records are siloed from direct public access.

4. Third-Party Services

Lynctel does not share data with advertising networks. Information is shared only with partners executing direct operational tasks — payment gateways, delivery networks, and SMS providers — under strict data processing agreements.

5. Your Controls & Rights

You retain control over your personal data on Lynctel:

Modification & Review: Edit your profile data in your account settings at any time.

Erasure Rights: Request permanent deletion of your personal data by contacting our support team.

6. Have Questions?

For security inquiries, technical transparency details, or data erasure requests, contact our team.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files — typically containing a unique identifier — sent from a website's server to your browser and stored on your device. They allow the platform to remember your actions or preferences over time. This technology is essential for features like secure logins and personalised settings.

2. Duration Classifications

We use both session and persistent cookies, each serving distinct roles:

Session Cookies

Temporary files that exist only during your active session and are deleted the moment you close your browser.

Persistent Cookies

Remain on your device after you close your browser. Used to recognise you on return visits and maintain preferences.

3. Why We Use Cookies

Cookies on Lynctel fall into four functional categories:

🔒

Essential (Strictly Necessary)

Critical for platform security, user authentication, and core features like maintaining your shopping basket.

📈

Performance & Analytics

Collect anonymous data on visitor interactions to help us improve speed and experience.

🛠️

Functional & Personalisation

Remember choices you make — such as language or region — to provide a more personal experience.

🎯

Targeting (Not Used for Ads)

Used by integrated partners for social sharing or maps. Lynctel does not share data with advertising networks.

4. Integrated Third-Party Cookies

To provide features like integrated payment gateways, delivery tracking maps, or social sharing widgets, we use trusted third-party services. These partners may place their own cookies via our platform. Since we do not control these technologies, we recommend reviewing their individual privacy and cookies policies.

5. Managing Your Consent & Settings

Your browser or device provides tools to block, delete, or manage cookies. Disabling Essential cookies will cause critical features like secure login and checkout to fail.

Most browsers accept cookies by default. You can modify your settings to decline them. Consult your browser's Help section for specific instructions:

Chrome Settings Firefox Options Safari Preferences Edge Configuration
